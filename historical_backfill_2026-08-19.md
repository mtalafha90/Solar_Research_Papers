### Scientific relevance

The paper quantifies a polar-flux threshold required to restart global polarity reversal after Maunder-like grand minima. It reports that the duration of a grand minimum is independent of the onset rate and does not control the recovery rate, while the absence of a robust precursor for entry into a minimum remains an open problem. This is relevant to nonlinear cycle regulation because it treats recovery as a thresholded dynamical transition rather than as a purely linear decay/restart process.
