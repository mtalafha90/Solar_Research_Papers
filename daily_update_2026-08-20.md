## New since the previous check

**HIGH PRIORITY — Understanding mechanisms underlying solar cycle predictability with a general framework**  
Binghang Li; Jie Jiang; Yukun Luo. Published online **20 May 2026**, *Astronomy & Astrophysics* 709, A220. DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202659373](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202659373). Preprint: [arXiv:2604.13585](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13585).

**Mechanism / relevance:** This paper establishes a Stokes-theorem-based criterion linking the surface induction integral to the net toroidal-flux generation rate in dynamo models. Across five representative models, the surface field becomes predictive when the surface poloidal field is efficiently coupled back into the dynamo loop through flux transport such as meridional circulation, and when the observed radial surface field represents the radial component of the interior poloidal field. The paper explicitly notes that predictive skill depends on the prescribed stochasticity and nonlinearity of the model, making it relevant to questions of how TQ/LQ/inflow feedbacks alter predictability rather than only amplitude. **Class: CORE / predictability-constraint.**

**Why HIGH PRIORITY:** It provides a model-independent diagnostic for testing whether a nonlinear SFT/BL model's surface field is physically connected to the next toroidal-cycle amplitude. This is useful for comparing TQ, LQ, inflow, decay, and Lorentz-force feedback implementations on equal footing.
